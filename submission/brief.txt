PolicyPulse 360 (Team Neural Mavericks) is a Customer 360 + Next Best Action copilot for Indian insurers. It unifies policy, claims, payment and call-centre data in Snowflake. Three modular Cortex Code CLI skills run alone or chained. $c360-unify builds a 1-row-per-customer 360 view. $interaction-intel turns call transcripts into sentiment (CORTEX.SENTIMENT on the customer's words), intent (AI_CLASSIFY) and one-line summaries (AI_COMPLETE). $next-best-action computes an explainable 0-100 churn score from 8 weighted drivers, then uses AI_COMPLETE, grounded on the 360 row and cited call IDs, to write the action, the reason and a ready-to-send message. A Streamlit app turns a question into an action: 360 card, risk gauge, evidence, copyable message, portfolio view with premium at risk, and plain-English Q&A. On 300 synthetic customers, it flags 26% as high risk with 87% recall and surfaces Rs 37 L of premium at risk. XS warehouse, incremental Cortex calls.
