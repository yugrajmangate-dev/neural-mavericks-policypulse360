PolicyPulse 360 (Team Neural Mavericks) is a Customer 360 + Next Best Action copilot for Indian insurers, built on Snowflake. It unifies policy, claims, payment and call-centre data. Four modular Cortex Code CLI skills run alone or chained. $c360-unify builds a 1-row-per-customer 360 view. $interaction-intel turns 819 call transcripts into sentiment, intent (AI_CLASSIFY) and one-line summaries (AI_COMPLETE). $next-best-action computes an explainable 0-100 churn score from 8 weighted drivers; then GPT-4.1 via Cortex AI_COMPLETE, grounded on the 360 row and cited call IDs, writes the action, the reason and a ready-to-send message. $ask-policypulse queries a Cortex Agent (Analyst on a semantic view + Cortex Search) that answers with cited transcripts. Streamlit and React apps turn a question into an action. On 300 synthetic customers it flags 22% as high risk with 81% recall at 91% precision, surfacing Rs 32.9 L of premium at risk and 41 high-risk renewals due within 30 days.
